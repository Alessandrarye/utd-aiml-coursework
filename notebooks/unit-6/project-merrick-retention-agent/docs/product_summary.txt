Product Summary — Merrick Bank Retail Offerings

1. Classic Credit Card
   - Annual fee: ₹500
   - Minimum credit limit: ₹25,000
   - Cash advance fee: 3% of amount withdrawn
   - Rewards: 1 point per ₹100 spent
   - Ideal for new-to-credit users.

2. Gold Credit Card
   - Annual fee: ₹1,000
   - Limit: ₹50,000–₹2,00,000
   - Reward rate: 2X on groceries, fuel, and dining.
   - Late payment fee: ₹350
   - Designed for working professionals with stable income.

3. Platinum Credit Card
   - Annual fee: ₹2,000
   - Limit: ₹2,00,000–₹5,00,000
   - Rewards: 5X on travel and e-commerce, lounge access benefits.
   - Eligibility: income above ₹8L per annum, credit score >750.

4. Personal Loan
   - Tenure: 1–5 years
   - Interest rate: 10–18%
   - Processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount
   - Early repayment penalty: 2%.

5. Savings Account
   - Minimum balance: ₹10,000
   - Interest rate: 3.5% per annum
   - Free debit card, 5 free ATM transactions per month.

6. Fixed Deposit
   - Tenure: 6 months – 10 years
   - Interest rate: 6.5% to 7.8% per annum
   - Premature withdrawal penalty: 1%.
