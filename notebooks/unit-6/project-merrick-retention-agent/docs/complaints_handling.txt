Complaints Handling — Policy and Process

1. Objective
   Ensure every customer complaint is acknowledged and resolved promptly, maintaining high satisfaction scores.

2. Response Timeline
   - Acknowledgment within 24 hours.
   - Resolution within 5 working days.
   - Complex cases may take up to 10 days.

3. Channels for Complaint
   - Toll-free number
   - Email support
   - Chatbot or live chat
   - Social media escalation

4. Classification
   - Category A: Billing, fees, interest disputes.
   - Category B: Service delay, staff behavior, or app malfunction.
   - Category C: Fraud or security issues (handled with highest priority).

5. Compensation
   - Billing errors: refund or reversal within 3 days.
   - System outage: service credits up to ₹200.
   - Fraud cases: provisional refund within 5 working days, subject to investigation.

6. Customer Assurance
   - Each complaint gets a unique tracking ID.
   - Weekly progress updates are shared via email or SMS.
   - Complaints unresolved beyond 10 days escalate automatically to the Nodal Officer.

7. Tone of Communication
   - Always start with an apology.
   - Avoid technical jargon.
   - Confirm satisfaction before closing the ticket.
