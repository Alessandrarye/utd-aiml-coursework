Fee Waiver Policy

1. Eligibility
   - Active customer for at least 12 months.
   - No late payment in the last 3 cycles.
   - Credit score >700 or verified financial hardship.

2. Types of Fee Waivers
   - Annual Fee Waiver: applicable once per card per 12 months.
   - Late Payment Waiver: one-time waiver for accidental delays within 5 days.
   - Over-limit Fee Waiver: for customers who pay full outstanding within the same billing cycle.

3. Approval Process
   - Auto-approval for eligible customers with tenure >24 months.
   - Manual review by retention team for all others.
   - Requests must be logged in CRM with justification.

4. Exclusions
   - Waivers not applicable for cash advance fees, EMI conversion fees, or reward redemption charges.

5. Communication Template
   “We appreciate your long association with us. As a gesture of goodwill, we have waived your recent ₹500 annual fee. Your loyalty means a lot to us.”

6. Record Keeping
   - All waivers must be recorded in the customer’s retention history.
   - Audit logs retained for 3 years.
