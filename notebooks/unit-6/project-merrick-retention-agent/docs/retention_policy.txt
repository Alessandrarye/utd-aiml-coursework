Retention Policy — Customer Engagement & Offers

Our primary goal is to retain valued customers by understanding their needs and offering customized solutions.

1. Retention Philosophy
   - Retention is driven by empathy and transparency.
   - Agents should focus on understanding pain points rather than offering immediate discounts.

2. Customer Segmentation for Retention
   - High-value customers: tenure > 3 years or credit score > 750.
   - Moderate-value customers: tenure 1–3 years or credit score between 650–750.
   - Low-value customers: tenure < 1 year or credit score < 650.

3. Retention Offers
   - High-value: fee waiver up to ₹1,000, bonus reward points, or interest reduction of 0.5%.
   - Moderate-value: 50% annual fee waiver or EMI interest reduction.
   - Low-value: free add-on card or personalized financial counselling.

4. Escalation Rules
   - Agents should never promise offers beyond policy limits.
   - Any exception for customers with special circumstances must be approved by the Retention Desk Manager.

5. Churn Prevention Signals
   - Negative feedback in the past 60 days.
   - Inactivity for more than 90 days.
   - Frequent service complaints.
   - Drop in average monthly balance or spend by >40%.

6. Retention Script Guidelines
   - Use the customer’s first name.
   - Acknowledge their concern directly.
   - Offer one relevant retention option.
   - End with a clear action (e.g., “I’ve applied a ₹500 waiver to your next bill.”).

7. Success Metrics
   - Retention rate improvement (target: +8% annually).
   - Average resolution time: < 10 minutes per call/chat.
