Rewards and Benefits — Program Overview

1. Earning Points
   - 1 reward point = ₹1 for redemption.
   - Classic Card: 1X on all spends.
   - Gold Card: 2X on groceries, fuel, and dining.
   - Platinum Card: 5X on travel, e-commerce, and luxury stores.

2. Bonus Campaigns
   - 20% extra points during festive seasons.
   - Double points for auto-debit bill payments.
   - Lifetime free cards for customers spending over ₹3 lakh annually.

3. Redemption Options
   - Pay with Points: directly adjust statement balance.
   - Gift catalogue (vouchers, electronics, etc.).
   - Airline miles or hotel loyalty transfers.

4. Reward Expiry
   - Points are valid for 2 years from earning date.
   - Expiry reminders are sent 30 days before lapse.

5. Dispute Resolution
   - Missing points must be reported within 45 days.
   - Once verified, points are re-credited within 7 working days.

6. Loyalty Tier System
   - Silver: default for all new customers.
   - Gold: spending above ₹1.5L/year → 5% bonus points.
   - Platinum: spending above ₹3L/year → 10% bonus points and 1 annual airport lounge access.
