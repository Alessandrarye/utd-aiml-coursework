# Frequently Asked Questions — Accounts & Credit Cards

1. How can I check my account balance or credit card limit?
Customers can check balances and credit limits via the Merrick Mobile App, Internet Banking, or by calling customer support. Balances are updated in real time.

2. What should I do if I miss a payment?
If a payment is missed, the system automatically applies a late fee of ₹350. Customers should make the pending payment immediately. If payment is made within 7 days, interest adjustments may be waived on request.

3. Can I change my billing date?
Billing date changes are allowed once every six months. Requests must be made through customer service. The new date takes effect from the next billing cycle.

4. What is the minimum due amount?
The minimum due is 5% of the total outstanding amount or ₹500, whichever is higher.

5. How can I close my account?
Customers may close an account after clearing all dues and EMIs. The request can be raised through the app or at any branch. Closure is confirmed via registered email.

6. How do I update my address or contact details?
Changes can be made online under 'Profile Settings' with OTP verification. For address updates, a valid government-issued proof of address is required.

7. How do I increase my credit limit?
Credit limit increases are offered to customers with a good payment record, usually after 12 months. Requests are reviewed automatically during monthly account assessment cycles.

8. What are the annual fees and interest charges?
Annual membership fees range between ₹500–₹2,000 depending on the card type. The monthly interest rate for unpaid balances is 3.25%.

9. What if there is a suspicious transaction?
The customer should immediately freeze the card using the mobile app and report the transaction through the 24x7 helpline. Investigations typically resolve within 5 working days.

10. How can I convert purchases into EMI?
Transactions above ₹3,000 are eligible for EMI conversion at a nominal interest rate starting at 1.25% per month. Customers can choose 3, 6, 9, or 12-month tenures.
